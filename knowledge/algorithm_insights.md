## Platform Retention Patterns

### YouTube Shorts
- Hook window: first 0–3 seconds are critical
- Algorithm reads swipe-away as negative signal
- Sub counts matter less than engagement rate for distribution
- Best retention curve: front-loaded hook → steady middle → clear CTA ending

### TikTok
- Re-watch rate weighted heavily (short videos benefit from loop-ability)
- Comments triggered by questions or disagreement outperform pure praise
- Sound selection: trending audio gives initial distribution boost

### Instagram Reels
- Shares and saves weighted higher than comments for Reels
- Hashtags have diminishing returns (3–5 targeted > 30 generic)
- Subtitle readability affects muted-viewing completion
